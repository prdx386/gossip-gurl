# Procurement signatures

How a client buys shows through the broker's text. A combination of constraints, broker count and timing often points to a handful of organisations before any search is run. This table is observed behaviour, DACH market, and will drift: check the date column, and add what you see.

| Signature in the posting(s) | Points to | Observed | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 to 5 brokers within 24 h, identical requirement list, "Freelancer und 1-Personen-Gesellschaften ausgeschlossen", "sozialversicherungspflichtig", onshore only | Deutsche Bahn group procurement (any DB subsidiary) | 2026-10 | high |
| Team request ("Teamanfrage", "im Team abzudecken"), 40 to 60 person-days, management presentation at year end | Group-level assessment commissioned by executive board or new executive; large regulated group | 2026-10 | medium |
| Single broker, GULP or Hays exclusive, matrix-style "nice-to-have" prose ("bis zu 18 Jahre", "6 Jahre (2+4)") | DAX or large Mittelstand with a framework contract; requirement text generated from a scoring sheet | 2026-10 | medium |
| Public hospital or university clinic, no broker, VgV language | Direct tender; brokers rarely involved; look on TED and state portals | 2026-10 | high |
| "Onshore innerhalb Deutschlands", "Projekt- und Dokumentationssprache Deutsch", security clearance or "SÜ2 vorhanden" | Federal or defence environment; brokers with BWI or Bundeswehr frameworks | 2026-10 | medium |
| TIBER-EU or DORA terms in a non-financial posting | Recycled template from a bank mandate, or client has a financial subsidiary; ask the recruiter | 2026-10 | low |

## How to use

1. After parsing, compare the posting's constraints and the mirror count against this table before building candidates. A match narrows the field immediately and tells you which other clues to trust.
2. A signature never names the client on its own. It tells you the family (DB group, public sector, DAX framework) and what to search for next.
3. When a case is resolved (see `feedback.md`), check whether its signature is in the table. If not, or if the date is stale, write the observation as a dated line into `notes.md` in the user's profile folder and read that file alongside this table on later runs. Do not edit this file at run time.

## Contributing a row

Rows reach this table through the repository (CONTRIBUTING.md). Add only what you observed across at least two postings or one confirmed case. Keep the "Points to" column at the level of group or sector, never a person or a named subsidiary under procurement. Date every row.
